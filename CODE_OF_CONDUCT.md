# Vectoris Engineering Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors, maintainers, and operators of the **Vectoris Workstation**, we pledge to foster an inclusive, collaborative, and highly professional engineering culture focused on craft, security, and technical excellence.

---

## Standards of Technical Craft

We commit to:

- **Mathematical Integrity:** Writing honest, robust, and mathematically sound takeoff and estimating algorithms without rounding shortcuts or hidden discrepancies.
- **Data Confidentiality:** Treating user data, architectural blueprints, and proprietary project drawings as strictly confidential and local-first by default.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Prioritizing system stability, memory safety, and deterministic failure recovery over rapid visual shortcuts or unverified mockups.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Reviewing architecture proposals constructively, offering actionable technical feedback, and maintaining uncompromising code standards.
- **Transparent Communication:** Practicing honest engineering communication without fabricated benchmarks, artificial progress bars, or simulated telemetry.

---

## Enforcement & Leadership

The Vectoris Engineering Leadership team is responsible for clarifying and upholding these standards across all repositories, issue trackers, and technical forums.

For inquiries or code standard discussions, reach out to the core engineering maintainers at `engineering@vectoris.ai`.
